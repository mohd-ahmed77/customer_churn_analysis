The Strongest Churn Signal: Contract Type

6.7× Higher Churn Risk

Monthly subscription contracts exhibit a dramatically higher churn rate compared to annual commitment plans.

Strategic Interpretation

  • Monthly plans present extreme churn exposure and low switching costs.
  • Annual contracts provide a strong operational retention anchor.
  • Priority: Target monthly users with annual upgrade incentives.
